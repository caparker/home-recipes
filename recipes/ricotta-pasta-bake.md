## Ingredients

- 1 lb short pasta (penne, rigatoni, or ziti)
- 10 oz frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed very dry
- 15 oz whole-milk ricotta
- 1 large egg
- 2 cups (8 oz) shredded low-moisture mozzarella, divided
- 1 cup (2 oz) grated Parmesan, divided
- 3 cups (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 3 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt (plus more for pasta water)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Optional: 2 cups broccoli florets, cut small

## Equipment

- 9x13 baking dish
- Large pot for pasta
- Colander
- Mixing bowl
- Box grater or microplane

## Prep-ahead notes

Assemble fully up to 24 hours ahead, cover, refrigerate. Add 10 min to bake time if starting cold. Freezes well before baking: wrap tightly, freeze up to 2 months; thaw overnight in fridge before baking.

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Lightly oil a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook pasta 2 minutes less than package directions (it will finish in the oven). If using broccoli, add florets to the pasta water for the final 2 minutes. Drain.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine ricotta, egg, 1 cup mozzarella, 1/2 cup Parmesan, garlic, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, pepper, and olive oil. Stir in squeezed-dry spinach until evenly mixed.
4. Spread 1 cup marinara across the bottom of the baking dish.
5. Add drained pasta (and broccoli, if using) to the ricotta mixture along with remaining 2 cups marinara. Stir gently until pasta is fully coated.
6. Transfer to baking dish. Top with remaining 1 cup mozzarella and 1/2 cup Parmesan.
7. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Uncover and bake 10–15 more minutes until bubbling and top is golden.
8. Rest 5–10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Squeezing the spinach dry is essential — wet spinach = watery bake.
- Broccoli version: works best when florets are cut small (~1/2 inch) so they cook through and blend with the pasta texture.
- Toddler tip: portion a small amount before adding red pepper flakes.
- Leftovers reheat well; add a splash of water and cover to keep from drying out.
- For a richer version: swap 1/2 cup of the ricotta for mascarpone.
