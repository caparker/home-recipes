## Ingredients

- 14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 10 oz fresh spinach (or 1 lb frozen, thawed)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 green chili, minced (optional)
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional)
- 1/3 cup heavy cream (or coconut milk)
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1 tbsp butter
- Juice of 1/2 lemon

## Equipment

- Blender
- Large skillet
- Chef's knife

## Prep-ahead notes

Spinach purée and tofu can be prepped 1–2 days ahead and stored separately in the fridge. Full dish keeps 3 days refrigerated or 2 months frozen (freeze before adding cream for best texture).

## Instructions

1. Press tofu 15 minutes, then cube into 3/4-inch pieces.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in skillet over medium-high. Pan-fry tofu until golden on 2–3 sides, about 6 minutes. Remove and set aside.
3. Blanch spinach in boiling water 30 seconds, then transfer to ice water. Drain and blend to a smooth purée with 2 tbsp water.
4. In same skillet, heat remaining 1 tbsp oil. Sauté onion until soft, 5 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, and chili; cook 1 minute.
5. Add tomato and spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric, cayenne). Cook 3–4 minutes until tomato breaks down.
6. Stir in spinach purée and 1/4 cup water. Simmer 5 minutes.
7. Add tofu, cream, salt, and garam masala. Simmer 5 minutes.
8. Finish with butter and lemon juice. Adjust salt.

## Notes

- Toddler tip: reserve plain cubed tofu and a small scoop of rice before the spicy sauce.
- For a richer flavor, swap butter for ghee.
- Serve over jasmine rice or with naan.
- Freezes well — omit cream, add fresh when reheating.
