## Ingredients

- 1 small green cabbage (about 1.5 lb), cored and thinly sliced
- 2 medium carrots, grated
- 1/2 small red onion, very thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup buttermilk
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp celery seed
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Equipment

- Chef's knife or food processor with slicing disc
- Large bowl
- Whisk

## Prep-ahead notes

Improves with time. Make at least 1 hour ahead, up to 24 hours. The chill softens the cabbage and melds flavors. Toss once more before serving.

## Instructions

1. Combine cabbage, carrots, and red onion in a large bowl.
2. Whisk mayonnaise, buttermilk, vinegar, mustard, sugar, celery seed, salt, and pepper in a separate bowl until smooth.
3. Pour dressing over vegetables and toss thoroughly to coat.
4. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour, up to 24 hours, tossing once more before serving.
5. Taste and adjust salt/vinegar before serving.

## Notes

- Food processor slicing disc turns this into a 5-minute prep job.
- For a lighter version: swap half the mayo for plain Greek yogurt.
- Toddler tip: pull a small portion of shredded cabbage + carrot aside pre-dressing; toddlers often prefer plain crunchy veg.
