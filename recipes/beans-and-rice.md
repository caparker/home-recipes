## Ingredients

- 2 cans beans, drained and rinsed (black + pinto recommended)
- 1/2 yellow onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper (red or green), diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, minced (plus 1 tsp adobo sauce)
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano (optional)
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme (optional)
- 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional)
- 2 cups rice (jasmine or brown), cooked
- Juice of 1 lime
- Olive oil, salt, pepper
- ~1 cup water or veg broth

## Equipment

- Rice cooker
- Large skillet or dutch oven
- Chef's knife

## Prep-ahead notes

Beans reheat beautifully — make a double batch and freeze half in flat portions. Rice can be cooked in the AM and rewarmed. Full dish keeps 4 days in fridge.

## Instructions

1. Start rice in rice cooker.
2. Heat olive oil in skillet over medium. Sauté onion and bell pepper with a pinch of salt, 5–7 min until soft.
3. Add garlic, smoked paprika, oregano, thyme, and cayenne. Stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add beans, chipotle + adobo, bay leaves, and ~1 cup water or broth. Simmer 15–20 min until thickened, stirring occasionally.
5. Remove bay leaves. Stir in lime juice. Taste and adjust salt.
6. Serve beans over rice.

## Notes

- **Suggested addition:** 1 tsp ground cumin with the other spices — big flavor boost, very traditional.
- **Toddler tip:** Reserve plain beans + rice before adding chipotle (spicy). Toddler already loves beans, so this is a slam-dunk base.
- **Bean combo:** Black + pinto is classic. Black + white beans also works for a milder flavor.
- **Stretch it:** Top with avocado, cheese, salsa, cilantro, or fried egg. Wrap leftovers in tortillas for burritos.
- **Chipotle heat:** Freeze remaining canned chipotles in 1-pepper portions on parchment.
