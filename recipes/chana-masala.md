# Chana Masala

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp neutral oil (veg or olive)
- 1 tbsp cumin seeds (whole — see notes)
- 2 shallots, finely diced (about 1 cup)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chili powder (or 1/2 tsp cayenne for more heat)
- 1 (28 oz) can whole or crushed tomatoes
- 3 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed (reserve 1/2 cup liquid)
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Fresh cilantro, for serving

## Method

1. Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and toast until fragrant and sizzling, about 30 seconds.
2. Add shallots and cook, stirring, until soft and golden at the edges, 5–7 minutes.
3. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Stir in coriander, turmeric, and chili powder. Toast in the oil for 30 seconds — this blooms the spices.
5. Add tomatoes (crush by hand if whole). Simmer 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce darkens and oil separates at the edges.
6. Add chickpeas and 1/2 cup reserved chickpea liquid (or water). Simmer 15 minutes, mashing some chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken.
7. Stir in garam masala, sugar, lemon juice, and salt. Taste and adjust — it should be tangy, slightly sweet, and well-salted.
8. Top with fresh cilantro. Serve over rice or with naan.

## Notes

- **Cumin seeds vs. ground:** whole seeds bloomed in oil give much more depth. If using ground cumin, add it with the other spices in step 4 instead.
- **Make-ahead:** Better on day 2. Keeps 5 days refrigerated, 3 months frozen.
- **Toddler tip:** Cut chili powder to 1/4 tsp; the flavor is still great. Serve with plain rice and a dollop of yogurt to cool.
- **Thickness:** Mashing chickpeas is the secret to that clingy, restaurant-style sauce. Don't skip it.
