## Ingredients

### Tofu
- 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed (3/4-inch)
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (peanut or vegetable)
- Pinch salt

### Vegetables (mix and match, ~6 cups total)
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 carrots, thinly sliced on the bias
- 1 cup snap peas or green beans
- 4 scallions, cut into 1-inch pieces (whites and greens separated)

### Aromatics
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated

### To serve
- 1 batch stir-fry sauce (see =pairs_with=)
- Cooked jasmine or brown rice
- Toasted sesame seeds (optional)

## Equipment

- Large skillet or wok
- Rice cooker
- Chef's knife
- Tofu press or clean towels + weight

## Prep-ahead notes

- **Tofu:** press up to a day ahead; cube and store covered. Can also be pan-fried ahead and reheated in the pan or oven.
- **Vegetables:** wash, cut, and store in containers up to 2 days ahead — stir-fries live and die by mise en place.
- **Rice:** cook earlier in the day; reheat with a splash of water.
- **Sauce:** mix ahead (see individual sauce recipes).

## Instructions

1. **Press tofu** for at least 20 minutes (or use a tofu press). Cube into 3/4-inch pieces. Toss with cornstarch and a pinch of salt.
2. **Cook rice** in the rice cooker while you prep.
3. **Sear tofu:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add tofu in a single layer and cook undisturbed 3-4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Transfer to a plate.
4. **Stir-fry vegetables:** Add another splash of oil. Start with the hardest vegetables first (carrots, broccoli stems), then medium (bell pepper, broccoli florets), then quick-cooking (snap peas, scallion whites). Cook 4-6 minutes total, stirring frequently, until crisp-tender.
5. **Add aromatics:** Push veg to the side, add garlic and ginger to the empty spot, cook 30 seconds until fragrant, then stir into the vegetables.
6. **Return tofu to pan.** Pour in sauce and toss to coat. Simmer 1-2 minutes until glossy and thickened.
7. **Finish** with scallion greens and sesame seeds. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- **Toddler tip:** reserve a portion of plain tofu + steamed broccoli + rice before saucing. Toddler loves the crispy cubes.
- **Vegetable swaps:** mushrooms, bok choy, cabbage, zucchini, edamame all work. Avoid watery veg like tomatoes.
- **Heat matters:** if the pan isn't screaming hot, you'll steam instead of stir-fry. Cook in batches if needed.
- **Cornstarch tofu trick:** gives that crispy takeout-style exterior — worth the extra minute.
- **Sauce rotation:** see =pairs_with= for six sauce options. Brown sauce is the default; peanut-ginger for kid nights; coconut-curry when you want more of a saucy dinner over rice.
