## Ingredients

**Roasted tofu and squash**
- 14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 lb delicata squash (1 large or 2 small), halved, seeded, cut into 1/2-inch half-moons (skin on)
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt

**Curry**
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1/4 tsp chili powder (see notes for more heat)
- 1 (14.5 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 (14 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/4–1/2 cup water, as needed
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 cups jasmine rice, cooked (for serving)
- Optional garnishes: fresh cilantro, plain yogurt, lemon wedges

## Equipment

- Chef's knife
- Sheet pan
- Large pot or Dutch oven
- Rice cooker

## Prep-ahead notes

- Press tofu and cut squash up to 2 days ahead; store separately in fridge.
- Curry base (through step 7) can be made a day ahead; reheat and fold in tofu/squash before serving.
- Full curry keeps 4 days refrigerated, 2 months frozen. Squash softens on reheating; freeze the base alone for best texture.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss tofu with 1 tbsp oil, soy sauce, and cornstarch. Spread on a parchment-lined sheet pan and roast.
2. Toss squash with 1 tbsp oil and 1/4 tsp salt. After the tofu has roasted 8 min, add squash to the pan (or a second pan). Roast another 18–22 min, flipping halfway, until squash is tender and browned and tofu is golden.
3. Meanwhile, cook jasmine rice in rice cooker per usual.
4. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large pot over medium. Add cumin seeds and toast until fragrant and sizzling, about 30 seconds.
5. Add onion; cook 5–7 min until soft and golden at the edges. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 min.
6. Stir in coriander, turmeric, and chili powder. Toast in the oil 30 seconds.
7. Add crushed tomatoes. Simmer 8 min, stirring occasionally, until the sauce darkens and oil separates at the edges. Add coconut milk, chickpeas, brown sugar, and salt. Simmer 10 min, mashing some chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken. Add water if it gets too thick.
8. Off heat, stir in garam masala and lemon juice. Taste and adjust salt/lemon.
9. Fold in roasted tofu and squash just before serving — don't simmer the squash or it turns to mush.
10. Serve over jasmine rice with garnishes.

## Notes

- Heat: 1/4 tsp chili powder is toddler-mild. Use 1/2 tsp for medium, 1 tsp for hot. Paprika instead of chili powder gives color with no heat.
- Ground cumin works in place of seeds; add 1 1/2 tsp with the coriander in step 6.
- Toddler tip: reserve plain roasted squash and tofu before combining; serve sauce on the side with yogurt.
- Butternut or acorn squash work too; peel butternut and roast 25–30 min with the tofu.
- Add 2 cups baby spinach in step 8 for extra veg.
