## Ingredients

- 1 cup jasmine or basmati rice
- 1/2 cup red lentils (masoor dal), rinsed
- 8 oz paneer, cubed
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp red chili powder (optional)
- 2.5 cups water or veg broth
- 1 cup full-fat coconut milk
- 3–4 cups fresh baby spinach (or 1 cup thawed frozen)
- 2 tbsp butter or oil
- 1 tsp salt
- Fresh cilantro + lime wedges to serve

## Equipment

- Deep skillet or Dutch oven with lid
- Small skillet (for paneer)
- Chef's knife

## Prep-ahead notes

Rinse lentils and cube paneer in the morning. Full dish reheats well 2–3 days in the fridge; add a splash of water or coconut milk when warming. Freezes okay but paneer texture softens.

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in a deep skillet or Dutch oven. Add cumin seeds; sizzle 30 seconds.
2. Add onion, cook 5 minutes until soft. Add garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute more.
3. Stir in turmeric, garam masala, coriander, chili powder (if using), and salt.
4. Add rinsed rice and lentils; stir to coat in the spices.
5. Pour in water/broth and coconut milk. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce heat. Simmer 20 minutes.
6. Meanwhile, pan-fry paneer cubes in a little oil until golden on 2 sides (~5 minutes). Set aside.
7. When rice is tender, stir in spinach and fold in paneer. Cover and rest off heat for 3–5 minutes to wilt the spinach.
8. Fluff gently, top with cilantro, and serve with lime wedges and yogurt.

## Notes

- Toddler-friendly: mild, soft, easy to scoop. Skip chili powder for their portion — pull some out before adding it if serving spicy for adults.
- Coconut milk adds richness; for a lighter version use light coconut milk or drop to 1/2 cup.
- For a palak-style variation: blend spinach with a splash of water and stir in at step 4 instead of at the end.
- Pairs well with cucumber salad, mango chutney, or a dollop of yogurt raita.
