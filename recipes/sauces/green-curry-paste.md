## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1/2 tsp white peppercorns (or black)
- 4 fresh green Thai chiles (hot), or 2 jalapeños (mild), stemmed — adjust to taste
- 1 stalk lemongrass, tender core thinly sliced (about 2 tbsp)
- 1 (2-inch) piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 medium shallots, quartered
- 1 cup packed fresh cilantro (stems and leaves)
- 1/2 cup packed fresh basil leaves (Thai basil if available)
- 1 tbsp lime zest (from ~2 limes)
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

## Equipment

- Small dry skillet
- Food processor

## Prep-ahead notes

- Fridge: 1 week (shorter than red/yellow because of fresh herbs).
- Freezer: 4 months in 1-tbsp cubes. Color dulls slightly but flavor holds.
- 1 tbsp paste ≈ mild per 4 servings; 2 tbsp = medium.

## Instructions

1. Toast coriander, cumin, and peppercorns in a dry skillet 2–3 min until fragrant. Grind.
2. Add all ingredients to food processor: ground spices, chiles, lemongrass, ginger, garlic, shallots, cilantro, basil, lime zest, salt, soy sauce, and oil.
3. Process 3–5 min, scraping down frequently, until smooth. Add 1 tbsp water if needed to help blend.
4. Transfer to jar (fridge) or ice cube tray (freezer).

## Notes

- **Heat control:** Thai chiles are the heat source; jalapeños keep it mild and kid-friendly. Remove seeds/ribs for further heat reduction.
- Thai basil gives an anise note that's characteristic of true green curry — worth seeking out but regular basil works.
- Best in: green curry with tofu + eggplant + bamboo shoots, coconut soups, marinades for grilled tofu.
- Toddler tip: this paste has strong fresh herb flavor; toddlers may prefer yellow or massaman.
