## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp fermented black bean garlic sauce (e.g. Lee Kum Kee)
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1/4 cup water or vegetable broth

## Equipment

- Small bowl
- Whisk

## Prep-ahead notes

Keeps 1 week refrigerated. Whisk before using — cornstarch settles.

## Instructions

1. Whisk cornstarch into water until dissolved.
2. Add remaining ingredients and whisk until sugar dissolves.
3. Add to stir-fry in the last 1-2 minutes; simmer until glossy and thickened.

## Notes

- Bold and savory — the most "takeout-tasting" of the sauce options.
- Pairs especially well with eggplant, mushrooms, and firm tofu.
- Verify your black bean sauce brand is vegetarian (some contain oyster extract).
- Toddler tip: pretty salty and pungent — reserve toddler portion of stir-fry before saucing.
