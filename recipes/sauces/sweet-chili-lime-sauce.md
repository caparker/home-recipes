## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp sweet chili sauce (store-bought, e.g. Mae Ploy)
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice (fresh)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp ginger, finely grated

## Equipment

- Small bowl
- Whisk
- Microplane or fine grater

## Prep-ahead notes

Keeps 1 week refrigerated. Lime juice mellows over time — brighten with a fresh squeeze before serving if made ahead.

## Instructions

1. Whisk all ingredients together in a small bowl until combined.
2. Toss with stir-fry in the last minute of cooking, or drizzle over finished dish.

## Notes

- Store-bought sweet chili sauce is mild — kid-approved despite the "chili" name.
- Great with bell peppers, snap peas, and crispy tofu.
- Also works as a dipping sauce for tofu nuggets or spring rolls.
- Pantry note: keep a bottle of sweet chili sauce on hand — long shelf life.
