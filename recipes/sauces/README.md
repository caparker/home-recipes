- Quick comparison of your four curry pastes

| Paste     | Heat        | Flavor profile              | Best for                          | Toddler-friendly |
|-----------+-------------+-----------------------------+-----------------------------------+------------------|
| Yellow    | None–mild   | Earthy, warm, turmeric      | Everyday curries, lentil soups    | ★★★              |
| Massaman  | Almost none | Sweet-warm spices, cozy     | Potato/squash curries, stews      | ★★★              |
| Red       | Mild–medium | Bright, chile-forward       | Classic Thai curries              | ★★               |
| Green     | Medium–hot  | Herby, citrusy, fresh       | Green curry, marinades            | ★                |

- Additional shopping items for green + massaman

- /Produce:/ fresh green Thai chiles or jalapeños, cilantro (1 bunch), basil (Thai if available), extra limes, extra shallots
- /Pantry:/ cardamom pods, cinnamon sticks, whole cloves, whole nutmeg (or ground), brown sugar (already staple)
