## Ingredients

- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tbsp red or green curry paste (verify vegetarian — many contain shrimp)
- 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp lime juice (fresh, added at end)

## Equipment

- Small saucepan
- Whisk

## Prep-ahead notes

Keeps 4-5 days refrigerated. Reheats well — will separate slightly, whisk to recombine. Freezes okay but texture can get grainy on thaw.

## Instructions

1. Warm curry paste in a small saucepan over medium heat for 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Whisk in coconut milk, soy sauce, and brown sugar. Simmer 5-10 minutes to reduce and thicken slightly.
3. Off heat, stir in lime juice.
4. Pour over stir-fried tofu and vegetables; simmer together 1-2 minutes to marry flavors. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- Turns stir-fry into more of a saucy skillet/curry dish — plan for extra rice.
- Verify curry paste is vegetarian: Maesri and Mae Ploy have veg options; Thai Kitchen is reliably veg.
- Kid-friendly with green curry paste (milder) and less of it (start with 1 tbsp).
- Add a handful of spinach or peas at the end for extra veg.
- Pantry note: keep coconut milk and curry paste stocked — near-instant weeknight dinner.
