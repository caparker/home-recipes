## Ingredients

- 3 large dried guajillo or New Mexico chiles (mild), stemmed and seeded
- 1 tbsp coriander seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp cardamom pods (or 1/2 tsp ground), crushed
- 1 (3-inch) cinnamon stick, broken (or 1 tsp ground)
- 4 whole cloves (or 1/4 tsp ground)
- 1/2 tsp black peppercorns
- 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
- 1 stalk lemongrass, tender core thinly sliced (about 2 tbsp)
- 1 (2-inch) piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
- 6 cloves garlic
- 3 medium shallots, quartered (about 3/4 cup)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp neutral oil

## Equipment

- Small dry skillet
- Food processor

## Prep-ahead notes

- Fridge: 2 weeks. Freezer: 6 months in 1-tbsp cubes.
- This is the warmest, most stew-like paste — closer to Indian curry in spice profile.
- 1 tbsp per 4 servings = very mild; 2 tbsp = medium. Almost no chile heat.

## Instructions

1. Soak dried chiles in boiling water 15 min. Drain, reserving 2 tbsp liquid.
2. Toast coriander, cumin, cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and peppercorns in a dry skillet 2–3 min until fragrant. Grind with nutmeg.
3. Char shallots and garlic (skins on) in the dry skillet 5–7 min, turning, until blackened in spots. Peel. (Optional but classic — adds smoky depth.)
4. Combine all ingredients in food processor: soaked chiles, ground spices, lemongrass, ginger, charred garlic and shallots, tomato paste, salt, soy sauce, brown sugar, oil, and 2 tbsp chile water.
5. Process 3–5 min, scraping down, until smooth and thick.
6. Transfer to jar or ice cube tray.

## Notes

- **Heat control:** essentially no heat — the warmth comes from cinnamon, cardamom, and clove, not chiles. Great for toddler.
- Charring the alliums (step 3) is traditional and adds real depth; skip if short on time.
- Best in: massaman curry with potatoes + peanuts + tofu, coconut lentil stews, roasted squash.
- The most "Western-palate friendly" of the four pastes — think warm spices, mild sweetness, cozy.
- Pairs beautifully with peanuts: add 2 tbsp peanut butter or 1/4 cup crushed peanuts when using in a curry.
