## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced or grated
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1/4 cup water or vegetable broth
- Pinch red pepper flakes (optional)

## Equipment

- Small bowl
- Whisk
- Microplane or fine grater

## Prep-ahead notes

Best mixed fresh (garlic sharpens over time), but keeps 3-4 days refrigerated. Whisk cornstarch back in before using — it settles.

## Instructions

1. Whisk cornstarch into water until dissolved.
2. Add soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, sugar, and pepper flakes. Whisk to combine.
3. Add to stir-fry in the last 1-2 minutes of cooking; sauce will thicken and glaze the vegetables.

## Notes

- Lighter than the brown sauce — lets the vegetables shine.
- Good match for tender greens (bok choy, spinach) and mushrooms.
- Toddler tip: skip the pepper flakes or reserve toddler portion before adding.
