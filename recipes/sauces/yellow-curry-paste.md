## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp ground turmeric
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
- 1/4 tsp cayenne (optional; omit for no-heat version)
- 1 stalk lemongrass, tender core thinly sliced (about 2 tbsp)
- 1 (2-inch) piece fresh ginger, peeled and chopped
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 medium shallots, quartered
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp neutral oil

## Equipment

- Small skillet (optional, for toasting)
- Food processor

## Prep-ahead notes

- Same as red paste: 2 weeks fridge, 6 months frozen in 1-tbsp cubes.
- This paste is the milder, more toddler-friendly workhorse — use it as your default.

## Instructions

1. If using whole spices instead of ground: toast in dry skillet 2 min, then grind. Otherwise, skip to step 2.
2. Combine all ingredients in food processor. Process 3–5 min, scraping down, until smooth and thick.
3. Add 1–2 tbsp water if needed to help blend.
4. Transfer to jar or ice cube tray for freezing.

## Notes

- Turmeric gives color + earthiness; wear gloves or expect yellow hands.
- This is essentially a fresh-aromatic version of curry powder — much brighter than jarred.
- **Heat control:** cayenne is the only heat source; scale from 0 (baby-safe) to 1 tsp (adult medium).
- Great in: coconut curries, lentil soups, roasted vegetables, marinades for tofu.
