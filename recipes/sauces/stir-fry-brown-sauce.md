## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp vegetable bouillon
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp vegetarian oyster sauce (mushroom-based)
- 1 tsp garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp ginger, finely grated
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1-2 tsp rice vinegar (to taste)
- 1/4 cup water or vegetable broth
- 1 tsp cornstarch (optional, for thickening)

## Equipment

- Small bowl
- Whisk
- Microplane or fine grater

## Prep-ahead notes

Keeps in the fridge up to 1 week in a sealed jar. Shake before using. Doubles or triples easily; freezes well in small portions (ice cube tray works great).

## Instructions

1. Grate garlic and ginger into a small bowl.
2. Add soy sauce, bouillon, sesame oil, oyster sauce, brown sugar, and rice vinegar. Whisk until sugar and bouillon dissolve.
3. If using cornstarch, whisk it with the water separately, then stir into the sauce.
4. Add sauce to stir-fry during the last 1-2 minutes of cooking; it will thicken as it bubbles.

## Notes

- **Missing from original:** the recipe as written has no liquid volume for the pan — added 1/4 cup water/broth so the sauce actually coats the stir-fry rather than scorching.
- **Cornstarch tip:** 1 tsp cornstarch gives that glossy, cling-to-veggies texture typical of takeout brown sauce. Skip for a thinner sauce.
- **Sodium:** 1/4 cup soy + bouillon can read salty; taste before adding extra salt to your stir-fry.
- **Toddler tip:** pull some plain tofu/veg aside before adding sauce.
- **Batch idea:** make a 4x batch on Sunday, freeze in an ice cube tray — one cube ≈ one serving of stir-fry sauce.
