## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp peanut butter (natural, unsweetened)
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp ginger, finely grated
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1/4 cup warm water (adjust to desired consistency)

## Equipment

- Small bowl
- Whisk
- Microplane or fine grater

## Prep-ahead notes

Keeps 1 week refrigerated in a sealed jar. Will thicken when cold — thin with a splash of warm water before using.

## Instructions

1. Whisk peanut butter and warm water together until smooth.
2. Add soy sauce, maple syrup, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic. Whisk to combine.
3. Adjust water for pourable consistency (thinner for noodles, thicker for stir-fry glaze).

## Notes

- Rich and mild — great intro sauce for the toddler.
- For adults who want heat: add 1 tsp chili crisp or sriracha.
- Doubles as a dipping sauce for tofu nuggets or veggies.
