## Ingredients

- 8 oz dry elbow macaroni (or shells, cavatappi)
- 3 tbsp butter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups milk (whole or 2%), warmed
- 1/2 cup heavy cream (optional, for silkier sauce)
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Pinch of nutmeg (optional)
- 8 oz sharp cheddar, freshly grated
- 4 oz gruyère or Monterey Jack, freshly grated

### Optional topping (baked version)

- 1/2 cup panko
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- Pinch of salt

## Equipment

- Large saucepan
- Whisk
- Box grater
- Colander
- 8x8 or 2-qt baking dish (baked version)

## Prep-ahead notes

Sauce can be made and combined with cooked pasta a few hours ahead; keep covered in the fridge. Add a splash of milk when reheating or before baking. Assembled unbaked dish keeps 1 day refrigerated — add 10 min to bake time from cold.

## Instructions

1. Boil pasta 1–2 min shy of package directions. Drain and set aside.
2. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in flour and cook 1–2 min to a blonde roux (no color).
3. Slowly whisk in warm milk (and cream if using), a splash at a time, until smooth. Simmer 3–4 min, whisking, until it coats the back of a spoon.
4. Off heat, whisk in mustard, salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Add cheese in handfuls, stirring until smooth.
5. Fold in cooked pasta.
6. **To serve stovetop:** eat right away. Loosen with a splash of milk if it tightens.
7. **To bake:** transfer to a buttered 8x8 or 2-qt dish. Toss panko with melted butter and salt; scatter over top. Bake at 375°F for 20–25 min until bubbly and golden.

## Notes

- Grate your own cheese — pre-shredded contains anti-caking starch and won't melt as smoothly.
- Warm milk prevents lumps when building the sauce.
- Toddler add-ins: fold in steamed peas or small broccoli florets before serving/baking.
- Reheats well with a splash of milk over low heat or in the microwave, covered.
