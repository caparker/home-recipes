## Ingredients

- 1 medium delicata squash (about 1 lb), halved lengthwise, seeded, cut into 1/2-inch half-moons (skin on)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt (for beans)
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin (for beans)
- 1 tbsp lime juice (optional)
- 8 medium flour tortillas (8-inch)
- 2 cups shredded cheese (Monterey Jack, cheddar, or a mix), about 8 oz
- 2 tbsp butter, for griddling (plus more as needed)

## Equipment

- Oven and sheet pan
- Chef's knife
- Large skillet or griddle
- Fork or potato masher
- Mixing bowl

## Prep-ahead notes

- Squash can be roasted up to 3 days ahead; store in the fridge.
- Bean-squash mash can be made 2 days ahead.
- Assembled, uncooked quesadillas freeze well: stack with parchment between, wrap, freeze up to 2 months. Cook from frozen over medium-low, covered, until heated through and crisp (about 4 min per side).

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss delicata half-moons with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp cumin, chili powder, and black pepper. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan.
2. Roast 20–25 minutes, flipping once halfway, until tender and caramelized at the edges.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine black beans, grated garlic, 1/4 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp cumin, and lime juice. Mash with a fork until about half the beans are broken down but texture remains.
4. Add roasted delicata to the beans and lightly mash together — leave visible chunks of squash.
5. For each quesadilla: sprinkle 1/4 cup cheese on half of a tortilla, top with about 1/2 cup of the squash-bean mixture, add another 1/4 cup cheese, and fold the tortilla in half.
6. Melt about 1 tsp butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook quesadilla 2–3 minutes per side, pressing lightly, until golden and the cheese is melted. Repeat, adding butter as needed.
7. Let rest 1 minute, then cut into wedges.

## Notes

- Toddler tip: before mashing, set aside a small portion of plain roasted squash and plain black beans. Many toddlers prefer the components separate.
- For a spicier adult version: add pickled jalapeños or a smear of chipotle in adobo inside the quesadilla.
- Good toppings: sour cream, salsa, avocado, cilantro, hot sauce.
- If delicata is very sweet, add an extra pinch of salt and a squeeze of lime to the mash to balance.
- Swap: butternut squash works but needs to be peeled; roast time is similar.
