## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1.5 tsp baking soda
- 1.5 tsp baking powder
- 1.25 tsp kosher salt
- 2.5 cups buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 3 tbsp melted butter (plus more for cooking)

## Equipment

- 2 mixing bowls (one large, one medium)
- Whisk
- Griddle or large nonstick/cast-iron skillet
- 1/4-cup measure or ladle

## Prep-ahead notes

- Dry mix can be combined the night before and left covered on the counter.
- Batter itself is best mixed fresh, but benefits from a 20–30 min rest before cooking.
- Cooked pancakes freeze well: cool on a rack, freeze flat on a sheet tray, then bag. Reheat in toaster or microwave.

## Instructions

1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter until smooth.
3. Pour wet into dry and stir just until combined — lumps are fine. Do not overmix.
4. Let batter rest 20–30 minutes at room temp (this is when the baking soda + buttermilk really activate).
5. Heat griddle over medium; brush with butter. Ladle ~1/4 cup batter per pancake.
6. Cook until bubbles form and edges look set (~2–3 min), flip, and cook another 1–2 min until golden and cooked through.
7. Hold finished pancakes on a rack in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.

## Notes

- Overmixing = tough, chewy pancakes. Stop stirring while streaks of flour remain; they hydrate during the rest.
- Batter thickness: should slowly ribbon off the whisk. Too thick → thin with a splash of buttermilk; too thin → sprinkle in a tbsp of flour.
- Toddler tip: make silver-dollar-sized pancakes for easy self-feeding; freeze extras for weekday breakfasts.
- Add-ins (fold in gently after batter rests): blueberries, thin banana slices, mini chocolate chips.
- With this much baking soda + powder, don't skip the rest — but also don't push past ~45 min or you'll lose lift.
