## Ingredients

- 4 cups cooked jasmine rice, cold (day-old works best)
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots (or fresh diced carrots + peas)
- 1 cup broccoli florets, chopped small
- 3 scallions, thinly sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp white pepper (or black)
- Optional: 1 tsp chili crisp

## Equipment

- Wok or large skillet
- Chef's knife
- Rice cooker (for base rice)

## Prep-ahead notes

Rice must be cold — cook rice 1 day ahead and refrigerate uncovered for 30 min before storing. Vegetables can be chopped up to 2 days ahead. Whole dish reheats fine but best fresh.

## Instructions

1. Break up cold rice with your fingers so grains separate.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in wok over medium-high. Pour in eggs, scramble softly 30 seconds, then transfer out.
3. Add remaining 2 tbsp oil. Sauté onion 2 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, and scallion whites; cook 30 seconds.
4. Add carrots and broccoli; stir-fry 3 minutes until crisp-tender. Add peas; cook 1 minute.
5. Push veg to side, add rice to open space, and press flat. Let sear 1 minute, then toss.
6. Drizzle soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and white pepper over rice. Toss to coat evenly.
7. Return eggs to pan, add scallion greens, and toss briefly.
8. Taste, adjust soy/salt. Serve with chili crisp on the side.

## Notes

- Cold rice is non-negotiable — warm rice turns to mush.
- Toddler tip: pull out a portion after step 5 (before soy sauce) for a low-sodium version.
- Great for using up any leftover veg: bell peppers, corn, cabbage all work.
- Add cubed pan-fried tofu for extra protein.
