## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup (113 g) butter, softened
- 1/2 cup (100 g) white sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup (250 g) buttermilk
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 cup (140 g) cornmeal
- 1 cup (120 g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Equipment

- 8x8 baking dish or 9-inch cast-iron skillet, greased
- 2 mixing bowls
- Hand mixer or whisk

## Prep-ahead notes

- Keeps 2 days covered at room temperature. Freezes well in squares up to 2 months; reheat in a 350°F oven 8–10 minutes.
- Day-old cornbread reheats better with a little butter on top.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease the pan.
2. Cream butter and sugar until light.
3. Beat in eggs one at a time.
4. Stir baking soda into the buttermilk, then mix into the batter.
5. Add cornmeal, flour, and salt. Stir until just combined; don't overmix.
6. Pour into the pan and bake 25–30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Cool 10 minutes before cutting.

## Notes

- This is a sweet, cake-style cornbread. Variations below assume that and keep the sugar unless noted.
- Melted butter works instead of creaming; the crumb is slightly denser.

## Variations

### Muffins
Divide batter among 12 lined muffin cups. Bake at 375°F for 18–22 minutes.

### Cheddar
Fold 1 cup (4 oz) shredded sharp cheddar into the finished batter. Optional: 2 thinly sliced scallions. Sharp cheddar against the sweet base works like honey-cheddar cornbread. For a more savory lean, drop sugar to 1/3 cup (65 g).

### Roasted squash
Mash 1 cup (about 200 g) leftover roasted squash flesh smooth with a fork and beat in after the eggs. Reduce buttermilk to 3/4 cup (190 g) to offset the squash's moisture. Add 1 tsp baking powder with the dry ingredients so the heavier batter still rises. Keep the sugar; the squash adds body more than sweetness. Bake time is the same; the center takes a touch longer, so check with a toothpick. Untested as written.

### Cheddar and squash
Combine the two variations above. Good in muffin form for the PM snack rotation, and as a side for chili or beans. Untested as written.
