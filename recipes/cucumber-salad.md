- Cucumber Salad

* Ingredients
- 1.5 lb (about 2) English cucumbers, or 6 Persian cucumbers
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (unseasoned)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp chili crisp or 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced

* Method
1. Place cucumbers on a cutting board and smash with the flat of a knife until they crack. Tear into rough 1-inch pieces.
2. Toss cucumbers with the kosher salt in a colander set over a bowl. Let drain 10 minutes, then gently squeeze out excess water.
3. Whisk vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and garlic in a bowl until sugar dissolves.
4. Add cucumbers and toss to coat. Top with chili crisp, sesame seeds, and scallions.
5. Serve within 30 minutes for best texture.

* Notes
- Salting step is essential; skipping = watery salad.
- Not truly make-ahead — dress just before serving.
- Toddler tip: reserve plain smashed cucumbers before dressing.
