## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb (about 2) English cucumbers, or 6 Persian cucumbers, cut into 1/2-inch dice
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (unseasoned)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 1 tsp chili crisp or 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced

## Equipment

- Chef's knife
- Colander
- Mixing bowl
- Whisk

## Prep-ahead notes

Not truly make-ahead — dress just before serving. Cucumbers can be diced and salted up to a few hours ahead; keep drained cucumbers covered in the fridge and dress at the last minute.

## Instructions

1. Dice cucumbers into roughly 1/2-inch pieces.
2. Toss cucumbers with the kosher salt in a colander set over a bowl. Let drain 10 minutes, then gently press or pat dry to remove excess water.
3. Whisk vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, and garlic in a bowl until sugar dissolves.
4. Add cucumbers and toss to coat. Top with chili crisp, sesame seeds, and scallions.
5. Serve within 30 minutes for best texture.

## Notes

- Salting step is essential; skipping = watery salad.
- Dicing gives a cleaner, more toddler-friendly texture than smashing (uniform bites, no jagged shards).
- Toddler tip: reserve plain diced cucumbers before salting/dressing.
- For a lighter/less salty version: reduce soy sauce to 2 tsp and add 1 tsp extra vinegar.
