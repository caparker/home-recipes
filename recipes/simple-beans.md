## Ingredients

- 2 cans beans (pinto, black, or a mix), undrained
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp vegetable bouillon
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 tsp baking soda (softens beans)
- Salt, to taste
- 1 tbsp olive oil (optional, for sautéing garlic)

## Equipment

- Saucepan
- Wooden spoon

## Prep-ahead notes

Keeps 4–5 days in the fridge; freezes well for up to 3 months. Flavor deepens overnight. Reheat gently with a splash of water.

## Instructions

1. (Optional) Warm olive oil in a saucepan over medium; sauté garlic 30 seconds until fragrant.
2. Add beans (with their liquid), bouillon, water, and baking soda.
3. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until beans are soft and liquid has thickened slightly.
4. Mash lightly with a spoon if you want a creamier texture.
5. Salt to taste and serve.

## Notes

- Sautéing the garlic first (rather than boiling raw) makes a big flavor difference.
- Add a bay leaf or a pinch of cumin/oregano for more depth.
- Toddler tip: pull some out before salting; mash for younger eaters.
- Double the batch — freezes beautifully in flat quart bags for future taco/burrito/quesadilla nights.
- For pinto: finish with a squeeze of lime. For black: a dash of smoked paprika.
