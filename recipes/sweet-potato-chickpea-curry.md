## Ingredients

- 14 oz extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp peanut or vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 lb sweet potatoes (about 2 medium), peeled and cut into 3/4-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 3 tbsp red curry paste (or 2 tbsp for milder/toddler)
- 1 (14 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup vegetable broth or water
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp lime juice (about 1/2 lime)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 2 cups jasmine rice, cooked (for serving)
- Optional garnishes: fresh cilantro, sliced scallions, lime wedges, chili crisp

## Equipment

- Chef's knife
- Sheet pan
- Large pot or Dutch oven
- Rice cooker

## Prep-ahead notes

- Press tofu and cube sweet potatoes up to 2 days ahead; store separately in fridge.
- Curry base (through step 5) can be made a day ahead; reheat and add tofu/lime before serving.
- Full curry keeps 4 days refrigerated, 2 months frozen. Flavor improves overnight.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Press tofu 15 min, then cube. Toss with 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp soy sauce, and 1 tbsp cornstarch.
2. Spread tofu and sweet potato cubes on a sheet pan (line with parchment). Roast 25–30 min, flipping halfway, until sweet potatoes are tender and tofu is golden.
3. Meanwhile, cook jasmine rice in rice cooker per usual.
4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pot over medium. Sauté onion 5 min until soft. Add garlic and ginger; cook 1 min.
5. Add curry paste; stir 1 min until fragrant. Pour in coconut milk, broth, chickpeas, soy sauce, brown sugar, and salt. Simmer 10 min.
6. Fold roasted sweet potatoes and tofu into the curry. Simmer 2–3 min to combine.
7. Off heat, stir in lime juice. Taste and adjust salt/lime.
8. Serve over jasmine rice with garnishes.

## Notes

- Toddler tip: reserve plain roasted sweet potato and tofu cubes before combining; toddler loves finger foods and this avoids curry heat.
- For milder version: use 2 tbsp curry paste (or yellow curry paste, which is milder).
- Add 2 cups baby spinach at the end for extra veg — wilts in ~1 min.
- Sub butternut squash for sweet potato if in season.
- Peanut butter variation: stir in 2 tbsp creamy peanut butter with the coconut milk for a richer, nuttier curry.
