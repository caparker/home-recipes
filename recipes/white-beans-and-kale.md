## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil, plus more for finishing
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh rosemary, minced (or 1/2 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1/2 tsp dried)
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 3 (15 oz) cans white beans (cannellini or great northern), drained and rinsed
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 parmesan rind (optional but recommended)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 bunch lacinato kale (about 6 oz), stems removed, leaves chopped
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Black pepper to taste
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Grated parmesan, for serving

### Optional add-ins

- 1 medium yukon gold potato, diced (add with broth)
- 1/2 cup small pasta (ditalini or orzo, add last 10 min)
- 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes (add with broth)
- Splash of heavy cream at the end for richness

## Equipment

- Dutch oven or large heavy pot
- Chef's knife
- Immersion blender (optional)

## Prep-ahead notes

Improves overnight. Keeps 4 days refrigerated or 2 months frozen. If freezing, undercook kale slightly. Reheat gently and refresh with a squeeze of lemon and drizzle of olive oil. If adding pasta, cook and store separately — it swells and gets mushy in leftovers.

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion, carrot, and celery. Cook 7–8 minutes until soft.
2. Add garlic, rosemary, thyme, and red pepper flakes. Cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Stir in tomato paste and cook 2 minutes, letting it darken slightly.
4. Add beans, broth, parmesan rind, bay leaf, and any optional add-ins (potatoes, tomatoes). Bring to a simmer.
5. Simmer uncovered 20 minutes to let flavors meld. (If using pasta, add during last 10 minutes.)
6. *Optional creaminess:* Remove parmesan rind and bay leaf. Blend 1–2 cups of soup with immersion blender directly in pot, or scoop out, blend, and stir back in.
7. Stir in kale and cook 3–5 minutes until wilted and tender.
8. Season with salt, pepper, and lemon juice. Taste and adjust.
9. Serve with a drizzle of olive oil and grated parmesan.

## Notes

- Parmesan rind adds huge depth — save rinds in the freezer for soups.
- Toddler tip: ladle a portion before adding red pepper flakes and lemon; kids often prefer the beans and carrots plain with a little broth and parmesan.
- For extra body without blending: mash 1 can of beans with a fork before adding.
- Curly kale works but is chewier — chop finer and simmer 2 minutes longer.
- Great with a hunk of crusty bread for dunking.
