## Ingredients

### Tofu
- 14 oz (400 g) extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into 3/4-inch (2 cm) cubes
- 1 tbsp (15 mL) peanut or vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp (15 mL) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (8 g) cornstarch

### Sauce
- 1 tbsp (15 mL) peanut or vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic (12 g), finely grated
- 1 tbsp (8 g) fresh ginger, finely grated
- 1.5 tbsp (10 g) yellow curry powder
- 1 can (400 mL / 13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
- 3 tbsp (48 g) creamy peanut butter
- 2 tbsp (30 mL) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (12 g) brown sugar
- 1 tbsp (15 mL) lime juice (or rice vinegar)

### Noodles & veg
- 8 oz (225 g) rice noodles (medium width, pad-thai style)
- 12 oz (340 g) broccoli, cut into small florets
- 2 medium carrots (150 g), cut into thin matchsticks
- 3 scallions (30 g), thinly sliced (whites and greens separated)

### To serve (optional)
- 2 tbsp (18 g) roasted peanuts, chopped
- Lime wedges
- Fresh cilantro

## Equipment

- Large nonstick or cast iron skillet
- Medium saucepan
- Colander
- Whisk
- Chef's knife

## Prep-ahead notes

- **Sauce** keeps 5 days refrigerated or 2 months frozen. Make a double batch.
- **Tofu** can be pressed and cubed a day ahead, kept dry in the fridge.
- **Vegetables** can be cut 1–2 days ahead.
- **Noodles** are best cooked just before serving — they get gummy if dressed too early. If prepping ahead, cook, rinse in cold water, toss with a few drops of oil, and reheat briefly in hot water before saucing.

## Instructions

1. **Press tofu** 15–20 minutes to remove excess moisture, then cube. Toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce, then dust with cornstarch until evenly coated.
2. **Sear tofu**: heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add tofu in a single layer and cook undisturbed 3–4 min per side until golden and crisp on at least 2 sides, ~10 min total. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Boil water** for noodles in a saucepan. Cook noodles per package directions (usually 4–6 min), add broccoli florets for the last 90 seconds to blanch. Drain, rinse briefly in cool water to stop cooking, and set aside.
4. **Build sauce**: in the same skillet, heat 1 tbsp oil over medium. Add scallion whites, garlic, ginger, and curry powder. Stir constantly 60–90 seconds until fragrant and the curry powder is toasted but not burned.
5. Add coconut milk, peanut butter, soy sauce, and brown sugar. Whisk until smooth. Simmer 3–4 min to thicken slightly.
6. Off heat, stir in lime juice. Taste and adjust: more soy for salt, more sugar for sweetness, more lime for brightness.
7. Add noodles, broccoli, carrots, and tofu to the skillet. Toss gently to coat.
8. Serve topped with scallion greens, chopped peanuts, cilantro, and lime wedges.

## Notes

- **Toddler portion**: before step 7, pull aside plain noodles, steamed broccoli, and a few tofu cubes. Offer a small spoon of sauce on the side for dipping if they're into it.
- **Mild by design** — yellow curry powder is warm, not hot. If an adult wants heat, pass chili crisp or sriracha at the table.
- **Carrot texture**: matchsticks stay crunchy when tossed in warm sauce. For softer carrots (toddler preference), add them to the skillet with the sauce in step 5 and simmer 2–3 min before adding noodles.
- **Peanut butter**: natural (just peanuts + salt) works best. If using sweetened/salted commercial PB, reduce brown sugar and soy slightly.
- **Noodle swap**: linguine or spaghetti works in a pinch — cook to al dente.
- **Protein swap**: chickpeas (1 can, drained, crisped in the skillet) instead of tofu is faster.
