## Ingredients

### Squash
- 1 medium delicata squash (about 1 lb), halved lengthwise, seeded, cut into 1/2-inch cubes (skin on)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

### Bean filling
- 2 (15 oz) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 clove garlic, finely grated
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack (or a Jack/cheddar mix)

### Enchilada sauce
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp chili powder (mild, like ancho-based)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 (8 oz) can tomato sauce
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt

### To assemble
- 12 corn tortillas (6-inch)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil (for warming tortillas)
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack (topping)
- Optional toppings: toasted pepitas, crumbled cotija, cilantro, sour cream, lime wedges

## Equipment

- Oven and sheet pan
- Chef's knife
- Medium saucepan (sauce)
- Blender or immersion blender
- Small skillet or comal (warming tortillas)
- Mixing bowl and fork or potato masher
- 9x13 baking dish
- Tongs

## Prep-ahead notes

- Squash can be roasted up to 3 days ahead; store in the fridge.
- Sauce keeps 5 days refrigerated, 3 months frozen.
- Bean-squash filling can be made 2 days ahead.
- Assembled, unbaked enchiladas can be refrigerated 24 hours; add 10 min to bake time.
- Freezer-friendly: assemble in a foil pan, wrap tightly, freeze up to 2 months. Thaw overnight before baking.

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss squash cubes with olive oil, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp cumin, chili powder, and black pepper. Roast in a single layer 20–25 min, flipping once, until tender and caramelized at the edges.
2. While the squash roasts, make the sauce: heat oil in a saucepan over medium, whisk in flour, and cook 1 min (don't brown). Whisk in chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, oregano, and cinnamon; cook 30 sec. Slowly whisk in broth, then tomato sauce and salt. Simmer 5–7 min until it coats a spoon. Blend smooth and adjust salt.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine black beans, garlic, 1/2 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp cumin, and lime juice. Mash until about half the beans are broken down.
4. Fold in roasted squash, lightly mashing so it holds together but keeps visible chunks. Stir in 1 cup cheese.
5. Lower oven to 375°F.
6. Heat a dry skillet or comal over medium. Brush each tortilla lightly with oil and warm 15–20 sec per side until pliable. Stack under a towel. *(Don't skip — cold corn tortillas crack.)*
7. Spread 1/2 cup sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 dish. Dip each tortilla briefly in sauce, spoon about 1/3 cup filling down the center, roll snugly, and place seam-down in the dish.
8. Pour remaining sauce over the top and sprinkle with remaining 1 cup cheese.
9. Cover with foil and bake 15 min. Uncover and bake 10 more min until bubbly and the edges brown.
10. Rest 5 min. Top with pepitas, cotija, and cilantro if using; serve with lime wedges.

## Notes

- Toddler tip: set aside a little plain roasted squash and plain black beans before mashing, or roll 1–2 tortillas with filling and cheese but no sauce.
- If the delicata is very sweet, add an extra squeeze of lime and a pinch of chipotle powder to the filling.
- Sauce shortcut: 2 cups store-bought red enchilada sauce works if pressed for time.
- Flour tortillas work too and don't crack: use 8 (8-inch) with about 1/2 cup filling each.
- Swap: butternut squash works but needs to be peeled; roast time is similar.
- Leftovers reheat well covered at 350°F for 15 min.
